## Prompts
- What were the three most significant events or decisions of this year?
- What am I most proud of achieving this year?
- What do I most regret not doing or starting?
- How did I grow as a person or professional this year?
- What relationships improved, and which need more attention?
- What did I spend time on that turned out not to matter?
- If I could give one piece of advice to myself at the start of this year, what would it be?
- What is the theme or word I want to define next year?
