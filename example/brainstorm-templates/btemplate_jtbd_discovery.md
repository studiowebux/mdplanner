## Questions
- What situation triggers the user to look for a solution?
- What job are they trying to get done — functional, social, and emotional?
- What were they using before, and why did that stop being good enough?
- What does success feel like for the user once the job is done?
- What obstacles or anxieties make them hesitate before switching?
- Who else is affected by the outcome of this job?
- What would make them recommend our solution to someone in the same situation?
- How does this job fit into a larger workflow or goal in their life?
