## Questions
- Who are our top three competitors and who do they primarily target?
- What is each competitor's core value proposition in one sentence?
- Where do competitors charge more, less, or differently than us?
- What features do they have that we lack, and do users actually care about them?
- What are the most common complaints users have about each competitor?
- Where are we clearly better, and are we communicating that advantage well?
- Which competitor is growing fastest and why?
- What would a customer who just switched from a competitor say was the deciding factor?
