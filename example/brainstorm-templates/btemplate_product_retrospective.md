## Questions
- What did we ship this cycle and are we proud of it?
- What was the biggest blocker and how did we work around it?
- Which decision do we wish we had made differently?
- What feedback from users surprised us most?
- What did we leave on the table that we should tackle next cycle?
- How well did our estimates match reality, and why?
- What process or tool would have made the team more effective?
