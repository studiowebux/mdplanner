## Questions
- What specific user pain does this feature solve?
- Who is the primary user and what is their context when they hit this problem?
- What does success look like 6 months after launch?
- What is the simplest version of this feature we could ship to validate the idea?
- What existing features or workflows does this touch or break?
- What are the top three reasons this idea might fail?
- What would make a user choose a competitor's solution instead?
- How will we measure adoption and impact after launch?
