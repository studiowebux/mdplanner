## Questions
- What are the top three things that could prevent this from shipping on time?
- What assumptions are we making that have not been validated yet?
- Which external dependencies (APIs, vendors, teams) could block us?
- What happens to our users if this goes wrong in production?
- What is the rollback plan if we need to revert quickly?
- Are there compliance, security, or privacy concerns we have not fully addressed?
- What does the worst-case scenario look like and how likely is it?
