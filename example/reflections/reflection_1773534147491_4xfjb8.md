## What did I ship this week?

Migrated the onboarding and reflections domains onto the v2 clean
architecture and wired their views into the shared SSE bus, so both now
live-refresh without a manual reload. Also landed the domain-view factory's
dynamic form-options hook.

## What blocked me or slowed me down?

A snake_case mismatch in the repository parse layer cost most of a day —
frontmatter keys round-tripped fine on write but dropped on read for two
fields. The v1→v2 migration script also left a couple of legacy directories
in place, which I had to relocate by hand.

## What did I learn that I did not know before?

The factory's `extractFormOptions` hook can drive form selects entirely from
config, with no per-domain rendering code. That makes "seed from a template"
selectors a few lines instead of a bespoke widget.

## What would I do differently next sprint?

Write the repository round-trip test before migrating a domain, not after.
The two fields I lost would have been caught immediately by a save-then-load
assertion.

## What should I carry forward or double down on?

The "load a sibling, match the pattern" discipline. Reading two existing
domains before writing the third caught naming and error-handling drift
before it reached review.
