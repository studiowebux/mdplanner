# Analytics Dashboard Strategy 2026

## Vision

- (vision_1) Turn raw project data into decisions teams can trust

## Mission

- (mission_1) Give every team leader a clear, real-time view of delivery health

## Goals

- (goal_1) Launch the analytics dashboard to all paid plans
- (goal_2) Drive 40% weekly dashboard adoption among admins
- (goal_3) Reduce reporting time for managers by half

## Objectives

- (obj_1) Render core charts in under 500ms
- (obj_2) Cover 12 standard metrics out of the box
- (obj_3) Ship CSV and PDF export

## Strategies

- (strat_1) Pure-SVG, theme-reactive charts for performance
- (strat_2) Opinionated default views over a blank canvas
- (strat_3) Drill-down from summary to record level

## Tactics

- (tactic_1) Instrument feature usage to find the most-viewed metrics
- (tactic_2) Monthly stakeholder review of dashboard requests
- (tactic_3) A/B test default chart layouts
