# Mobile App Strategy 2026

## Vision

- (vision_1) Be the fastest way to manage work from a phone

## Mission

- (mission_1) Bring the full power of the desktop planner to mobile without compromise

## Goals

- (goal_1) Ship native iOS and Android apps
- (goal_2) Reach 25,000 mobile installs
- (goal_3) Hit 4.5+ star rating on both stores

## Objectives

- (obj_1) Achieve sub-second cold start on mid-tier devices
- (obj_2) Support full offline-first sync
- (obj_3) Convert 15% of mobile installs to weekly active users

## Strategies

- (strat_1) Offline-first architecture as a differentiator
- (strat_2) Deep OS integration (widgets, share sheet, notifications)
- (strat_3) Phased rollout via TestFlight and Play beta tracks

## Tactics

- (tactic_1) Weekly beta builds to early adopters
- (tactic_2) In-app feedback prompts after key actions
- (tactic_3) Crash-free session monitoring with alerting
