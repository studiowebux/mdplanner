## Key Partners

- Apple App Store
- Google Play
- Firebase (push, crash reporting)
- RevenueCat (mobile billing)

## Key Activities

- Native client development
- Offline sync engine
- App store optimization
- Release management

## Key Resources

- Mobile engineering team
- Shared sync core
- Device test lab
- App store presence

## Value Proposition

- Full workspace, offline
- Instant cross-device handoff
- Native speed and gestures
- Secure biometric access

## Customer Relationships

- In-app onboarding tour
- Push re-engagement
- App store reviews and replies
- Beta tester community

## Channels

- App Store / Play Store
- In-app upsell from web
- Mobile demos and reels
- Shared-workspace referrals

## Customer Segments

- Field and on-site teams
- Travelling sales reps
- Mobile-first founders
- Remote workers

## Cost Structure

- Mobile engineering (55%)
- App store fees (15%)
- Push and crash infra (10%)
- QA and devices (20%)

## Revenue Streams

- Mobile Solo plan ($5/mo)
- Included in Team/Business tiers
- Offline storage add-on
