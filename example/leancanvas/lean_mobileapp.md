# Mobile App Lean Canvas

## Problem

- Desktop-only workflows leave field teams stranded
- No offline access when connectivity drops
- Push notifications are noisy and untargeted
- Switching devices loses in-progress context

## Solution

- Offline-first sync with conflict resolution
- Smart, batched notifications by priority
- Seamless handoff between desktop and mobile
- Biometric quick-unlock

## Unique Value Proposition

Your whole workspace in your pocket — fast, offline, and always in sync.

## Unfair Advantage

- Shared core engine with the web app (one codebase to evolve)
- Years of sync-conflict edge cases already solved
- Native performance via a thin platform layer

## Customer Segments

- Field and on-site teams
- Sales reps on the move
- Founders living out of their phone
- Remote workers without a fixed desk

## Existing Alternatives

- Mobile web (slow, no offline)
- Separate note apps (data silos)
- Email-to-self workarounds
- Competitor apps with thin mobile clients

## Key Metrics

- Mobile daily active users
- Offline session count
- Sync success rate
- Notification open rate

## High-Level Concept

"Linear's speed with Notion's depth, built mobile-first"

## Channels

- App Store and Play Store featuring
- In-app upsell from the web product
- Mobile-focused content and demos
- Referral via shared workspaces

## Early Adopters

- Existing web users who travel
- Sales teams piloting the beta
- Indie founders

## Cost Structure

- Mobile CI/CD and device farms
- App store fees
- Push infrastructure
- Mobile engineering time

## Revenue Streams

- Included in Team and Business plans
- Mobile-only Solo plan: $5/month
- Add-on offline storage tier
