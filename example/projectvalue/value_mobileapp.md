# Mobile App Value Board

## Customer Segments

- Field and on-site teams
- Travelling sales reps
- Mobile-first founders
- Remote workers without a fixed desk

## Problem

- Desktop-only workflows strand field teams
- No access when connectivity drops
- Context is lost when switching devices
- Notifications are noisy and untargeted

## Solution

- Offline-first sync with conflict resolution
- Seamless desktop ↔ mobile handoff
- Priority-batched, opt-in notifications
- Biometric quick-unlock

## Benefit

- Work anywhere, even offline
- Never lose in-progress context
- Stay focused with quieter alerts
- Faster, native-feeling interactions
